ABOUT US

Started by two people from Shivamogga

We began in 2019 by driving friends to the falls on weekends. Today we run small groups of eight or fewer, stay in family-run homestays, and pay our guides year-round rather than per trip. No coaches, no fifteen-stop days. One place at a time, long enough to notice it.

Sample plan

Monsoon Falls Circuit, day by day

An honest version, including the parts where nothing happens.

1

Day one — Shivamogga to Sagara

Pick-up at 8am, breakfast on the road, arrive at the plantation homestay by noon. Afternoon walk through arecanut and pepper. Dinner is akki rotti and local greens.

2

Day two — Jog Falls

Early start to reach the falls before the buses. Viewpoint, then the stepped path down if the rock is dry enough. Evening free at the homestay.

3

Day three — Sirsi and home

Marikamba temple, a coffee-roasting stop, and back to Shivamogga by 6pm.
